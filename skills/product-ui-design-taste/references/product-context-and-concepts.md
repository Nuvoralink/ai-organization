## Domain-expression pass

Produce these before drawing a major concept:

1. Five or more domain concepts: states, relationships, artifacts, movements, constraints, or vocabulary.
2. Five or more natural visual materials: color sources, physical surfaces, light, texture, geometry, instruments, maps, documents, traces.
3. Three category defaults to reject, including structural defaults.
4. One signature visual/spatial/data/interaction move.
5. Three manifestations of that signature across meaningful moments.

Examples of signatures:

- a deployment product whose environment topology is the persistent spatial spine;
- a support console whose conversation chronology and SLA pressure share one time field;
- a logistics tool whose route confidence and exceptions shape the map and list together;
- a dialer whose primary work card changes material and state in place as the live call progresses.

Avoid signatures that are only a gradient, border radius, logo, or novelty animation.
