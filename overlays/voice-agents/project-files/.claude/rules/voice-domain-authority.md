# Voice and AIL domain authority

The lighthouse is English outbound AIL insurance appointment booking in the US and Canada. The AI may explain the appointment purpose, gather booking fields, handle objections using grounded strategy, read constrained availability, confirm exact terms, book, or create a human handoff. It must not sell, advise, quote coverage, determine eligibility, or impersonate a licensed agent.

Only a normal-drain playout receipt advances played speech. Interruptions clear queued audio and never infer that unheard content reached the caller. Objection recovery is bounded and progresses through the vertical state machine. CoachAI may supply optional strategy methodology; transcripts supply scenarios only, never target replies.
