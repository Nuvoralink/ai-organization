Per Amin (2026-06-15): the per-call **rep Call Review** must stay **to-the-point and immediately actionable** — problem → "say this instead" → drill, easy to understand and implement. NO triad of explanations / "why it matters" essays on the per-call surface. The deeper "why it matters, the technique rationale, the longitudinal pattern" belongs in the **Improvement Plan** — that is the surface built to explain reasoning. Two surfaces, two altitudes: **Call Review = the fast fix for THIS call; Improvement Plan = the why + the cross-call pattern.**

Concretely drove (approved mock 2026-06-15, Approach A): removing the 3-column Problem / What-to-do / Why-it-matters block + the `whyItMatters` double-print from `TheMomentSection`; tightening the spine to headline → one consequence line → exchange → say-this-instead → drill → "before your next call"; adding score-in-context (vs team avg / personal best / trend) to `CallReviewHero`.

**Why:** reps act *between* calls and won't read a magazine spread; over-explaining on the per-call surface buries the one fix that matters. The explanatory depth already has a dedicated home (the Improvement Plan).
**How to apply:** when building or critiquing any per-call rep coaching surface, cut explanatory padding and push "why/rationale/pattern" depth to the Improvement Plan; keep the Call Review to the actionable fix. See [[gtm-readiness-threshold]].
