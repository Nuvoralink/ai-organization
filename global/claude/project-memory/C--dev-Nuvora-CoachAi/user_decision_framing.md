When designing systems or recommending architectures, lead with capability and cost. Mention risks/constraints honestly but do not let risk-aversion shape the recommendation.

**Why:** User explicitly said "i want you to think and design based on capability and cost, not out of fear or risks, mention them but dont limit the thinking and design." Quoted preference, not inferred.

**How to apply:** When the user asks for a design or research synthesis, structure the answer around (1) what's possible, (2) what it costs, (3) tradeoffs and risks as a separate section. Don't water down the recommended path because it has compliance/legal/ToS friction — surface that as context, let the user decide.
