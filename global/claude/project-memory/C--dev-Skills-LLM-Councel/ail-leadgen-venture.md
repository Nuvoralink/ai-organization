Amin is a FORMER American Income Life (AIL / Globe Life) insurance agent and now runs a business serving AIL agents. This is the domain behind the repo's insurance-themed council + studio runs.

**What he does today:** paid **appointment-setting** for AIL agents, working the **consent-based leads AIL provides** (he does NOT cold-call — hates it; the AIL leads carry their own consent).

**What he's building:** his own **consent-based, opt-in lead generation** for the products AIL sells (final expense / whole life, term, critical illness, accident) so he controls lead quality instead of depending on AIL's increasingly poor leads. Model: a pain-led ad names the real product → person fills a short INTENT-ONLY form (who it's for, what to cover — NEVER health/eligibility/coverage-amount, which are the licensed agent's job) → documented consent → one licensed independent agent calls. No insurer logos. Honesty (vs the usual "free benefit" bait) is the deliberate differentiator.

**Key facts established (his first-hand knowledge):** AIL is captive on PRODUCTS only — agents may buy/work outside leads, and may self-market with AIL legal approval + no AIL logo. Agents told him they'll pay for good appointments, good leads, AND new hires (recruiting).

**Council verdicts:** the full lead-business one-way run → Park-then-validate; after his corrections (permission resolved, opt-in not cold) the three-line run (leads / appointments / **recruiting**) → Pursue-with-changes @ 0.67. The council flagged **recruiting-as-a-service** as the lowest-legal-risk wedge. The `/studio` tool ([[studio-tool]]) was built to generate this venture's ad/landing assets; its first real run produced the final-expense Meta campaign and caught Meta's mandatory Financial-Products Special Ad Category as a launch blocker.
